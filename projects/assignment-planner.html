Adaptive assignment planner

An AI-assisted planning concept that turns an assignment into a realistic timeline and learns from how long tasks actually take.

AI Task planning Time estimation

The problem

Assignments often look manageable until the research, implementation, testing and writing tasks are broken down. Estimating those tasks accurately is difficult, especially when several deadlines overlap.

Proposed workflow

  • Enter an assignment brief and due date.
  • Generate a task breakdown and estimated timeline.
  • Mark sections complete and record their actual completion times.
  • Use that history to improve future estimates for the user.

Current stage

This is a project concept. The scheduling and adaptive estimation features still need to be designed and implemented.
