Cycloidal joint robot arm

A desk-mounted articulated arm exploring printed cycloidal joints, stepper-driven motion and camera-based visual servoing.

SolidWorks 3D printing Stepper motors Computer vision

Overview

The arm is being designed as a modular desk tool. Its initial application is carrying a lamp, with camera and gripper attachments considered for later versions.

The joint design explores cycloidal gearing to provide reduction while keeping the mechanism compact and suitable for prototyping with 3D-printed parts.

Design goals

  • Smooth, controlled joint movement.
  • Compact, serviceable printed gearboxes.
  • Interchangeable end effectors.
  • Visual servoing to track a target and guide the arm.

Current stage

The mechanical joints and gearing are in design. Visual servoing is a planned capability, not a completed feature.
