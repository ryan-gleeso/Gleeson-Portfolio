MediSort

A simulated pill-sorting system exploring how mechanical handling, sensing and control can work together.

Simulation Sensing Control

Overview

MediSort explores the design of a system that identifies and sorts pills. The project was developed in simulation rather than as a finished physical device.

Engineering focus

  • Handling individual items consistently.
  • Using sensor information to support sorting decisions.
  • Coordinating the sequence of detection and movement.
