Competition / Team project

Warman Challenge robot

A team-built competition robot that placed second at UTS.

Mechanical design Prototyping Teamwork

Overview

Our team designed, built and tested a robot to complete the Warman Challenge task. The project required translating a competition brief into a working mechanical solution.

What the project involved

  • Concept development and mechanical design.
  • Prototype fabrication and assembly.
  • Testing and refinement against competition requirements.
  • Working as a team under practical constraints.

Result

The robot placed second at UTS.
